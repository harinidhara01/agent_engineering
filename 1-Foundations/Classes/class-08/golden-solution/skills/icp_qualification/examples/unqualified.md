# Example: DO_NOT_QUALIFY

**Account:** Bright Leaf Financial Advisors — financial_services, 300 employees, United States. No known challenges or source notes relevant to plant modernization.

**Outcome:** DO_NOT_QUALIFY

**Rationale:** Industry (financial_services) is an explicit exclusion — this alone is sufficient to disqualify, independent of employee count. Employee count (300) is also below the minimum (5,000). Both are stated as independent reasons; a reader should not need to guess which one mattered.

This example illustrates: exclusion overriding any need to evaluate a pain signal at all — step 1 of the procedure ends the evaluation before step 3 would even apply.

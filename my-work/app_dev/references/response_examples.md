# Customer Response Examples

## Purpose

This document provides examples of the expected customer-facing communication style for the Home Service Concierge.

This file is **read-only**.

Agents should use these examples as style guidance.

Do not copy example content when it is not applicable to the customer's request.

---

# Communication Style

Customer-facing responses should be:

* Clear
* Concise
* Professional
* Friendly
* Direct
* Easy to understand

Avoid:

* unnecessary technical terminology
* excessive explanations
* internal agent terminology
* unsupported claims
* overly formal language
* unnecessary repetition

---

# Service Request Confirmation

Example:

> Thanks! I understand that you need HVAC service for an AC cooling issue in Columbus. I'll check available providers for your requested time.

---

# Missing Information

Example:

> I can help find a provider. What is the service location?

Another example:

> What day and time would you prefer for the service?

Ask only for information that is actually required.

---

# Provider Recommendations

Example:

> I found a few providers that match your request. Here are the best options:

**ABC HVAC Services**
Rating: 4.8
Estimated price: $120
Distance: 4.2 miles
Available: Tomorrow at 6:00 PM

> Recommended because it is available during your requested time and has the highest rating among the nearby options.

---

# Alternative Provider

Example:

> **Cool Air Experts**
> Rating: 4.7
> Estimated price: $110
> Distance: 6.1 miles
> Available: Tomorrow at 7:00 PM

> A good alternative with a slightly lower estimated price.

---

# No Matching Providers

Example:

> I couldn't find an available provider that matches your request for that time. You can try a different appointment time.

---

# Provider Evaluation Failure

Example:

> I wasn't able to evaluate all available providers, but I found the following options based on the providers I could verify.

Do not claim that all providers were evaluated if some evaluations failed.

---

# Booking Confirmation

Example:

> Your service is booked.

**Provider:** ABC HVAC Services
**Service:** AC repair
**Date:** Tomorrow
**Time:** 6:00 PM
**Location:** Columbus, OH

> Your confirmation number is BKG-001.

---

# Booking Failure

Example:

> I couldn't complete the booking. Your appointment has not been confirmed.

Do not tell the customer that a booking succeeded unless the booking operation actually succeeded.

---

# Important Communication Rules

1. Do not expose internal agent names to the customer unless the UI explicitly requires them.
2. Do not expose internal reasoning.
3. Do not invent provider details.
4. Do not invent booking details.
5. Do not claim an appointment is confirmed until the booking succeeds.
6. Keep explanations concise.
7. Match the tone and structure shown in these examples.
8. Adapt the examples to the actual customer request rather than copying them literally.

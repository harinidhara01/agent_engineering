# WidgetWare SDR Business Brief

## Executive Summary

WidgetWare provides software and services to manufacturing and industrial-automation companies to help them modernize plant operations and adopt AI-enabled automation.

## Offerings & Target Buyers

1. **Plant Operations Platform**: Connects plant-operational information and enables process monitoring.
   - *Target Buyers*: VP of Manufacturing, Plant Operations Director, Chief Digital Officer.
2. **Industrial AI Accelerator**: Enables governed AI use case identification and implementation.
   - *Target Buyers*: CTO, VP of Digital Transformation, Head of Industrial AI.

## Plausible Target Accounts (ICP Fit)

- **Scale**: Minimum 5,000 employees.
- **Industries**: Manufacturing, industrial automation, automotive manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, industrial equipment.
- **Excluded Industries**: Consumer retail, restaurants, personal services.
- **Regions**: United States, Europe, India.
- **Key Buying Signals**: Active plant modernization programs, new AI leadership appointments, digital transformation initiatives, GenAI hiring, manufacturing data consolidation projects.

## SDR Process & Human Approval Boundaries

The WidgetWare SDR process converts raw account leads and evidence into structured qualification analyses. 

**Human Approval Boundary**: The SDR system operates strictly as an advisory analysis tool. It **must stop** before performing any external action. Sending emails, posting social messages, modifying CRM records, or making pricing/contractual statements explicitly require human approval and oversight.
